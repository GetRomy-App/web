# Rōmy

> Rōmy helps small nonprofits find new major donors at a fraction of the cost of existing solutions.

Rōmy is a donor intelligence platform built for small nonprofit teams. It uses AI-powered prospect research to surface wealth indicators, giving histories, and affinity signals — then distills them into actionable donor profiles.

Key facts:

- Name: Rōmy (written with a macron over the "o"; often typed "Romy")
- Category: nonprofit prospect research, donor intelligence, and AI wealth-screening software
- Who it is for: small nonprofits — development directors, major gift officers, executive directors and boards without a dedicated prospect researcher or an enterprise wealth-screening contract
- Pricing model: no enterprise contracts or per-seat licensing (see "How We Built a Donor Intelligence Engine Starting at $0.10 per Prospect" below)
- Independent benchmark: PIF-Bench v2, an open, rubric-graded benchmark of AI on nonprofit development work, published by Rōmy Labs at https://getromy.app/labs

## Features

- Donor Discovery: AI-powered prospect research with wealth indicators, giving history, and affinity signals
- Fraction of the Cost: No enterprise contracts or per-seat licensing — donor intelligence at a price built for small nonprofits
- Ready to Act On: Every prospect comes with connection points, giving capacity, and outreach angles
